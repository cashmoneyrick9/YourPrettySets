# How It Works UX Pass Design

## Goal

Apply the best UX idea from the approved Baseline Improved mockup to the live `How It Works` section without doing a full UI polish pass yet.

This is a behavior and layout pass. The section should feel more natural under the hero, but final colors, final imagery, final copy, and detailed card styling are intentionally saved for a later UI pass.

## Current Status

- Built: a compact one-card step carousel directly under the hero.
- Approved: the section belongs near the hero and should explain the simple press-on process.
- Not approved yet: the current arrows, dots, and basic card-carousel feeling.

## CEO Judgment

- Judge next: whether the new section UX feels better on the live page.
- Main question: does the section feel like a calm, compact, image-led transition instead of a basic click-through card?
- Decision type: UX structure and motion, not final UI polish.

## Selected Direction

Use the Baseline Improved direction as the UX reference:

- compact rotating feature strip
- image-led
- three clear numbered steps
- no arrows
- no dot indicators
- informational only
- small peek of the next step
- calm automatic rotation
- stops rotating after shopper interaction

## Keep

- Same general placement under the hero.
- Compact height close to the current section.
- Three-step idea.
- Current placeholder visuals for speed.
- Placeholder copy for now.
- No CTA in this section.
- Page order and surrounding sections.

## Change

- Remove the visible arrow controls.
- Remove the dot indicators.
- Rearrange the existing visuals so the active step is more image-led.
- Add clear step numbers.
- Show one dominant step at a time.
- Let a small piece of the next step peek at the edge.
- Auto-rotate slowly and calmly.
- Stop auto-rotation once the shopper interacts with the section.
- Let the section feel wider or more edge-to-edge so it softens the move from hero to Collections.

## Do Not Touch

- Header or logo.
- Hero copy or hero image.
- Product sections.
- Collections behavior.
- Checkout or policy content.
- Final product photography.
- Final step copy.

## Creative Note

This section should make the press-on process feel easy, pretty, and beginner-friendly. The UX should be simple enough to understand without arrows, dots, or a large heading.

The section should not become a large feature block. It is still a compact bridge between the hero and shopping sections.

## UI Later

Save these for a later UI pass:

- new generated step imagery
- richer final image treatment
- final color polish
- final typography
- final card or strip styling
- final copy

## Mockup Notes

The Baseline Improved mockup was the useful direction. The other generated directions were less helpful because they looked too similar or drifted too far from the live section.

Future mockups for this section should stay strict:

- section-only
- compact like the live section
- based on the current live placement
- no full-page render
- no tall hero-like block
- soft hero-compatible container
- richer color inside the step visuals, not across the whole section background
